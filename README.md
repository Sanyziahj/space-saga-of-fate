# Space Riders: Space Saga of Fate - Golden Force vs Silver Force

A free side-scrolling space-board shooter, narrated by Torratio. Pick a rider from the Golden Force (Santt, ROS, Spike, Drill Sergeant) or the Silver Force (Overlord, Fright, Spark, The Red King), surf the cosmos and take down bosses that transform for the real fight. Light and Regular modes. Clear a campaign to earn a numbered Certified Victor poster.

Play: https://sanyziahj.github.io/space-saga-of-fate/
